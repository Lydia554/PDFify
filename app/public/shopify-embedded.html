💡 What is ZUGFeRD?

ZUGFeRD is a German standard for electronic invoices. It embeds an XML file directly into the PDF, allowing accounting systems like SAP and DATEV to automatically import invoice data without manual entry. Required for B2G (business-to-government) invoicing in Germany.

📜 What is PDF/A-3b?

PDF/A-3b is an ISO-standard format for long-term archival of documents. It ensures your invoices remain readable and legally valid for 10+ years, meeting German GoBD regulations for digital document storage.

❓ Frequently Asked Questions

What happens to the invoices after I generate them? ▼
Invoices are downloaded to your device as PDF files. We don't store your invoices - you're responsible for storing them according to your local regulations (e.g., 10 years in Germany under GoBD).
Can I customize the invoice template? ▼
Yes! You can customize the accent color, company name, and invoice language. We're working on more advanced template customization features.
Do I need ZUGFeRD for my business? ▼
If you sell to German government agencies (B2G), ZUGFeRD is required. For B2B, many German companies prefer it for automated accounting. It's becoming the standard for electronic invoicing in Europe.
What if I run out of pages? ▼
You can purchase token packs (1,000, 5,000, or 10,000 pages) that never expire. These add to your monthly limit, so you'll never be blocked from generating invoices. Alternatively, upgrade to Premium or Pro for higher monthly limits.
Is my customer data secure? ▼
Absolutely. We process orders in memory only and don't store customer data. All data is transmitted via HTTPS/TLS encryption. See our Privacy Policy for details.
Can I cancel my subscription anytime? ▼
Yes! You can cancel your Premium or Pro subscription at any time from your Shopify admin panel under Settings > Billing > Apps. You'll continue to have access until the end of your billing period.
What's the difference between Premium and Pro? ▼
Both include PDF/A-3b and ZUGFeRD. Premium gives you 1,000 pages/month. Pro gives you 10,000 pages/month plus priority support and bulk CSV processing for large-scale operations.

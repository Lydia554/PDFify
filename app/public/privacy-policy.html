Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

1. Who We Are

PDFify is a service operated by Lidija Jokić, providing PDF generation and invoicing tools for e-commerce stores.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Email address and password (for account login)
  • Shopify store domain and access tokens (to connect your store)
  • Stripe customer and subscription IDs (for payment processing)
  • API usage statistics (counts, previews, subscription type)
  • Consent to cookies and privacy terms

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate invoices, packing slips, and reports based on your Shopify orders
  • To manage authentication and user sessions
  • To process payments via Stripe
  • To monitor system usage and enforce limits
  • To send optional email communication (e.g., reset password, usage notifications)

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (to provide the PDFify service)
  • Legal obligations (e.g., tax regulations via Stripe)
  • Your explicit consent (cookies, marketing, analytics)

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to maintain user sessions and optionally to analyze traffic. No marketing cookies are used. You must explicitly consent to cookies before login. You can revoke this consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser.

6. Who We Share Data With

  • Shopify: To receive and process your store data
  • Stripe: For secure payment processing
  • MongoDB Atlas: For encrypted cloud database storage

7. Processing of Merchants' Customer Data

Important for Shopify Merchants: When you use PDFify to generate invoices, we process your customers' personal data on your behalf.

7.1 What Customer Data We Process

To generate PDF invoices, we temporarily access:

  • Order Information: Order ID, date, line items, pricing, taxes
  • Customer Information: Name, email address, shipping address, billing address
  • Store Information: Store name, logo, branding preferences

7.2 How We Process Customer Data

We do NOT store your customers' data. Our process is:

  1. Merchant requests an invoice → We fetch order data from Shopify API
  2. Data is processed in computer memory to generate a PDF invoice
  3. PDF is delivered to the merchant for download
  4. All data is immediately deleted from memory after PDF generation

Note: Merchants are responsible for storing and retaining downloaded invoices according to their local legal requirements (e.g., 10 years in Germany under GoBD regulations).

7.3 Our Role as Data Processor

For customer data processed through our Shopify app:

  • Merchant is the Data Controller (you control your customers' data)
  • PDFify is the Data Processor (we process data only on your instructions)
  • We process customer data solely to generate PDF invoices
  • We do not use customer data for marketing, analytics, or any other purpose
  • We do not sell or share customer data with third parties

7.4 Merchant Consent and Authorization

How Merchants Grant Consent: When you install PDFify from the Shopify App Store:

  1. Shopify displays a permission screen showing exactly what data PDFify will access
  2. You must explicitly click "Install app" to grant permission
  3. This authorization acts as your consent to process your customers' data for invoice generation
  4. You can revoke consent at any time by uninstalling the app from your Shopify store

Note: As the merchant, you are responsible for ensuring that your use of PDFify complies with your legal obligations to your customers, including providing privacy notices and obtaining any required consents.

7.5 Data Security

  • Encryption in Transit: All data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • No Data at Rest: We don't store customer data, so there is no data to encrypt at rest
  • Secure Authentication: We use Shopify's OAuth for secure API access

8. Data Retention

PDFify User Data: User data is stored for the duration of your account. Deleted accounts are anonymized or purged after 30 days.

Merchants' Customer Data: We do not store customer data. All order/customer data is immediately deleted from memory after invoice generation. Merchants are responsible for storing downloaded invoices.

9. Your Rights

PDFify Users: You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data.

Merchants' Customers: Since we do not store customer data, you can exercise your data rights through the merchant's Shopify store or by contacting us.

To make a request, contact us at: pdfifyapi@gmail.com

10. Shopify Protected Customer Data Access Compliance

PDFify complies with Shopify's requirements for apps that access protected customer data. This section details how we meet each Level 1 and Level 2 requirement.

10.1 Level 1 Requirements

Minimum Data Processing

✅ We process only the minimum personal data required:

  • Customer Name: Required to display on invoices
  • Customer Email: Required for payment reference on invoices
  • Customer Address: Required for shipping/billing address on invoices
  • Order Details: Required to generate invoice line items

No additional customer data is accessed or processed beyond what is necessary for invoice generation.

Transparency About Data Processing

✅ We inform merchants what data we process and why:

  • This privacy policy explicitly lists all customer data accessed (Section 7.1)
  • Shopify App Store listing describes data processing purposes
  • Our Shopify OAuth permission screen shows requested scopes before installation

Limited Processing Purposes

✅ We limit processing to stated purposes:

  • Customer data is used solely to generate PDF invoices
  • Customer data is not used for analytics, marketing, or any other purpose
  • Customer data is not shared with third parties

Customer Consent

✅ We respect merchant and customer consent decisions:

  • Merchants provide explicit consent during app installation via Shopify OAuth
  • Merchants can revoke consent by uninstalling the app at any time
  • Upon uninstall, access tokens are immediately invalidated

Opt-Out of Data Sharing

✅ We do not sell or share customer data:

PDFify does not sell, rent, or share customer data with any third parties. No opt-out is necessary because no data sharing occurs.

Automated Decision-Making

✅ We do not use automated decision-making:

PDFify does not use profiling, scoring, or any automated decision-making that has legal or significant effects on customers. We only generate PDF invoices.

Data Protection Agreements

✅ We have privacy and data protection agreements:

  • This privacy policy serves as our data protection agreement
  • Section 7.3 defines our role as Data Processor and merchants as Data Controllers
  • Merchants accept these terms by installing and using the app

Data Retention

✅ We apply appropriate retention periods:

  • Customer Data: NOT STORED - processed only in memory and immediately deleted after PDF generation
  • Merchant Data: Stored for account duration; deleted 30 days after account deletion

Data Encryption

✅ We encrypt data in transit and at rest:

  • In Transit: All data transmitted via HTTPS/TLS 1.2+ encryption
  • At Rest: MongoDB Atlas provides encryption at rest for stored data
  • API Keys: Encrypted using AES-256-CBC before storage
  • Passwords: Hashed using bcrypt (one-way encryption)

10.2 Level 2 Requirements

Backup Encryption

✅ Our database backups are encrypted:

MongoDB Atlas provides automatic encrypted backups. All backup data is encrypted using the same AES-256 encryption as the primary database.

Test/Production Data Separation

✅ We keep test and production data separate:

  • Development uses separate MongoDB databases from production
  • Production customer data is never used for testing
  • NODE_ENV environment variable controls environment separation

Data Loss Prevention Strategy

✅ We have data loss prevention controls:

  • Minimal Data Storage: Customer data is not stored, eliminating the primary data loss risk
  • Authentication Required: All API endpoints require valid authentication
  • Input Validation: All user inputs are validated and sanitized
  • Rate Limiting: API usage limits prevent excessive data extraction
  • CORS Protection: Cross-origin requests restricted to authorized domains

Staff Access Limits

✅ We limit staff access to customer data:

PDFify is operated by a single individual (Lidija Jokić). There are no employees, contractors, or third parties with access to customer data. Since customer data is not stored, there is no database of customer information for staff to access.

Strong Password Requirements

✅ We require strong passwords for staff accounts:

  • Single-operator model (no shared staff accounts)
  • Admin accounts require passwords with minimum 8 characters
  • Two-factor authentication (2FA) is enabled for all admin access
  • Passwords are hashed using bcrypt with salt rounds

Access Logging

✅ We log access to personal data:

  • API Requests: All invoice generation requests are logged with timestamp, shop domain, and order ID
  • Authentication Events: Login attempts and token usage are logged
  • Access Attempts: Failed authentication attempts are logged for security monitoring
  • Logs are reviewed: System logs are reviewed regularly for suspicious activity

Security Incident Response Policy

✅ We have a documented incident response policy:

See Section 11 below for our complete Security Incident Response Policy.

11. Security Incident Response

Our Commitment: We take the security of your data seriously and have established procedures to detect, respond to, and recover from security incidents.

11.1 What is a Security Incident?

A security incident includes but is not limited to:

  • Unauthorized access to user accounts or merchant data
  • Data breach or exposure of personal information
  • Suspicious activity on our servers or databases
  • Malicious attacks on our infrastructure

11.2 Our Response Process

If a security incident is detected, we will:

  1. Immediately contain the incident to prevent further damage
  2. Investigate the scope and impact of the breach
  3. Notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery (or as required by law)
  4. Notify authorities if required by GDPR or other regulations
  5. Implement remediation measures to prevent recurrence

11.3 Preventive Measures

  • Access Logging: All API access is logged for monitoring
  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (MongoDB Atlas)
  • Strong Authentication: Passwords are hashed; access tokens are securely stored
  • Regular Updates: Dependencies are kept up-to-date for security patches
  • Separate Environments: Development and production data are kept separate

11.4 Report a Security Incident

If you suspect a security vulnerability or incident involving PDFify, please contact us immediately:

pdfifyapi@gmail.com

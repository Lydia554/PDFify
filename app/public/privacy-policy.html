Privacy Policy

Last updated: February 22, 2026

1. Who We Are

PDFify is a service operated by Lidija Jokić, providing PDF generation and invoicing tools for e-commerce stores.

2. What Data We Collect

  • Email address and password (for account login)
  • Shopify store domain and access tokens (to connect your store)
  • Stripe customer and subscription IDs (for payment processing)
  • API usage statistics (counts, previews, subscription type)
  • Consent to cookies and privacy terms

3. How We Use Your Data

  • To generate invoices, packing slips, and reports based on your Shopify orders
  • To manage authentication and user sessions
  • To process payments via Stripe
  • To monitor system usage and enforce limits
  • To send optional email communication (e.g., reset password, usage notifications)

4. Legal Basis for Processing

We process your data under the following legal bases:

  • Performance of a contract (to provide the PDFify service)
  • Legal obligations (e.g., tax regulations via Stripe)
  • Your explicit consent (cookies, marketing, analytics)

5. Cookies and Tracking

We use cookies to maintain user sessions and optionally to analyze traffic. No marketing cookies are used. You must explicitly consent to cookies before login. You can revoke this consent at any time by clearing cookies in your browser.

6. Who We Share Data With

  • Shopify: To receive and process your store data
  • Stripe: For secure payment processing
  • MongoDB Atlas: For encrypted cloud database storage

7. Processing of Merchants' Customer Data

Important for Shopify Merchants: When you use PDFify to generate invoices, we process your customers' personal data on your behalf.

7.1 What Customer Data We Process

To generate PDF invoices, we temporarily access:

  • Order Information: Order ID, date, line items, pricing, taxes
  • Customer Information: Name, email address, shipping address, billing address
  • Store Information: Store name, logo, branding preferences

7.2 How We Process Customer Data

We do NOT store your customers' data. Our process is:

  1. Merchant requests an invoice → We fetch order data from Shopify API
  2. Data is processed in computer memory to generate a PDF invoice
  3. PDF is delivered to the merchant for download
  4. All data is immediately deleted from memory after PDF generation

Note: Merchants are responsible for storing and retaining downloaded invoices according to their local legal requirements (e.g., 10 years in Germany under GoBD regulations).

7.3 Our Role as Data Processor

For customer data processed through our Shopify app:

  • Merchant is the Data Controller (you control your customers' data)
  • PDFify is the Data Processor (we process data only on your instructions)
  • We process customer data solely to generate PDF invoices
  • We do not use customer data for marketing, analytics, or any other purpose
  • We do not sell or share customer data with third parties

7.4 Data Security

  • Encryption in Transit: All data is transmitted using HTTPS/TLS encryption
  • No Data at Rest: We don't store customer data, so there is no data to encrypt at rest
  • Secure Authentication: We use Shopify's OAuth for secure API access

8. Data Retention

PDFify User Data: User data is stored for the duration of your account. Deleted accounts are anonymized or purged after 30 days.

Merchants' Customer Data: We do not store customer data. All order/customer data is immediately deleted from memory after invoice generation. Merchants are responsible for storing downloaded invoices.

9. Your Rights

PDFify Users: You have the right to access, correct, export, or delete your personal data.

Merchants' Customers: Since we do not store customer data, you can exercise your data rights through the merchant's Shopify store or by contacting us.

To make a request, contact us at: pdfifyapi@gmail.com

10. Security Incident Response

Our Commitment: We take the security of your data seriously and have established procedures to detect, respond to, and recover from security incidents.

10.1 What is a Security Incident?

A security incident includes but is not limited to:

  • Unauthorized access to user accounts or merchant data
  • Data breach or exposure of personal information
  • Suspicious activity on our servers or databases
  • Malicious attacks on our infrastructure

10.2 Our Response Process

If a security incident is detected, we will:

  1. Immediately contain the incident to prevent further damage
  2. Investigate the scope and impact of the breach
  3. Notify affected users within 72 hours of discovery (or as required by law)
  4. Notify authorities if required by GDPR or other regulations
  5. Implement remediation measures to prevent recurrence

10.3 Preventive Measures

  • Access Logging: All API access is logged for monitoring
  • Encryption: Data encrypted in transit (HTTPS/TLS) and at rest (MongoDB Atlas)
  • Strong Authentication: Passwords are hashed; access tokens are securely stored
  • Regular Updates: Dependencies are kept up-to-date for security patches
  • Separate Environments: Development and production data are kept separate

10.4 Report a Security Incident

If you suspect a security vulnerability or incident involving PDFify, please contact us immediately:

pdfifyapi@gmail.com

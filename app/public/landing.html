📄 What is PDFify

PDFify is a robust SaaS platform and Document Generation as a Service (DGaaS) that transforms structured data (JSON, HTML) into branded, professional PDFs—automatically and at scale.

Designed with developers and businesses in mind, PDFify combines an intuitive user experience with deep technical flexibility.

While optimized for Shopify via webhook integration, it supports any technology stack without relying on client-side libraries or complex design setups.

Why PDFify

It solves the common pain points of manual, inconsistent, and error-prone document creation by delivering reliable, customizable PDF generation — invoices, packing slips, reports, and more — with seamless API-first access, secure authentication, dynamic templates, and easy email delivery.

One API Call, Endless Possibilities

A simple POST request is all it takes. Provide your Shopify domain and Order ID to get a compliant PDF back instantly. WooCommerce and other platforms follow a similar, developer-friendly pattern. 

curl -X POST http://localhost:3002/api/shopify/invoice \
-H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
-H "Content-Type: application/json" \
-d '{ 
  "shopDomain": "your-store.myshopify.com",
  "orderId": "1234567890",
  "merchant": true
}'

A Modern, Pure Node.js PDF Engine

We've engineered a powerful, pure Node.js engine for creating our highly-compliant merchant invoices. Unlike traditional HTML-to-PDF converters that rely on slow, resource-heavy headless browsers, our new engine uses the blazingly fast pdf-lib library to programmatically build documents from the ground up. This approach gives us unparalleled control over the PDF structure, resulting in perfectly compliant, lightweight, and professional invoices every time.

Unmatched Compliance & Quality

The result is a "hybrid" invoice that's both human-readable and machine-parseable, meeting the highest standards for e-invoicing and long-term archiving.

The Anatomy of a Compliant Merchant Invoice:

  • PDF/A-3b Compliant: For long-term electronic archiving (ISO 19005-3).
  • ZUGFeRD 2.3 Embedded: Contains a machine-readable XML invoice for automated processing.
  • Branded & Professional: Includes your logo and store details automatically.

Developer Preview: Shopify Example

A simple `POST` request is all it takes. Provide your Shopify domain and Order ID, and get a compliant PDF back instantly. WooCommerce and other platforms follow a similar, simple pattern. 

curl -X POST http://localhost:3002/api/shopify/invoice \
-H "Authorization: Bearer YOUR_API_KEY" \
-H "Content-Type: application/json" \
-d '{ 
  "shopDomain": "your-store.myshopify.com",
  "orderId": "1234567890",
  "merchant": true
}'

🛠️ Connect Your WooCommerce Store to PDFify

✅ Step 1: Enable REST API

  • Go to WooCommerce → Settings → Advanced → REST API
  • Click "Add Key"
  • Name it something like PDFify Connector
  • Set permissions to Read/Write
  • Click "Generate API Key"

✅ Step 2: Copy Your Keys

  • Copy the Consumer Key and Consumer Secret
  • Keep them safe – you'll need them to connect PDFify

✅ Step 3: Paste into PDFify

  • Enter your store URL, Consumer Key, and Consumer Secret in the fields on the right panel
  • Click "Connect Store"
